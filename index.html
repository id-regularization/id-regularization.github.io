When look-alikes are convincing, identity has to do the separating.

Why identity matters

Hard negatives can look almost right.

The target and distractors below share the same overall appearance. The difference can come down to a backpack, a zipper, shoe construction, hairstyle, or which hand carries a tote.

Takeaway. A hard negative may satisfy the dominant description while differing only in the identity-grounding details that decide the match.

Score-shift decomposition

Hard-negative suppression is a major correction mechanism.

For base-misranked queries, IAPR usually enlarges the positive–negative margin through hard-negative suppression and direct positive–negative separation, rather than uniform positive amplification.

IAPR adds persistent identity-aware references during optimization, then removes the auxiliary branch at inference so retrieval continues with the original base-retriever scoring function.
